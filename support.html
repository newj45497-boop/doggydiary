Common questions

Where are my photos stored? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so keep a backup of photos you care about.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open My → DoggyDiary Pro → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DoggyDiary Pro.
