DoggyDiary Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

DoggyDiary is a private diary for your dog. We collect no personal information about you.

What stays on your phone

The photos you choose, your notes, your dog's name, breed and dates are stored only on your device. We do not upload them, and we have no account system, advertising or analytics.

Photos

DoggyDiary reads only the photos you pick with the system photo picker and copies them into the app's private storage. Deleting a photo, memory or album in the app removes that copy. The app does not read or store the location data of your photos.

Purchases

If you buy DoggyDiary Pro, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to verify your purchase; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status, and does not receive your name, photos or notes. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. When you use the Share button, the content goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
